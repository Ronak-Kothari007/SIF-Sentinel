SIF Sentinel

AI-Assisted Serious Injury & Fatality Precursor Detection & Prevention Engine

TECHNICAL BRIEFING & ARCHITECTURE DOSSIER
Executive Summary: SIF Sentinel is an enterprise safety decision-support platform designed for high-risk industrial environments (mining, energy, manufacturing, construction). It detects hidden precursors to Serious Injuries and Fatalities (SIF) from incident descriptions, safety observations, and near-miss logs before catastrophe strikes.

1. What SIF Sentinel Solves

Traditional safety management systems suffer from the "Heinrich's Pyramid Fallacy"—the outdated assumption that reducing minor trivial incidents (e.g. slips, paper cuts) proportionally reduces fatal accidents. Modern safety science proves that only ~20% of near-misses contain the precursor conditions that lead to fatalities (e.g., heavy machinery interaction, high-pressure releases, suspended loads, confined space entry, electrical lockout failures).

SIF Sentinel replaces slow manual triage with a hybrid Multi-Factor Decision Engine:

  • Dual-Layer NLP Classification: Extracts domain hazards (Electrical, Height, Heavy Equipment, Chemical), life-critical activities, and barrier health indicators from natural language narratives.
  • Deterministic Safety Rules Engine: Enforces non-negotiable safety rules (e.g., Lockout/Tagout, Confined Space permits, Fall Arrest compliance). Even if an ML probability is low, rule violations immediately escalate the incident to High Priority.
  • Composite Risk Scoring & Prioritization: Computes an explainable composite risk score ($0.00 - 1.00$) categorizing reports into HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW priority for immediate HSE officer triage.
  • Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Auditability: Provides side-by-side comparison between AI suggestions and safety officer verdicts, capturing feedback in an immutable, append-only audit trail.
  • Vector Semantic Similarity Search: Automatically surfaces historical near-misses and recurring hazards using vector cosine embeddings (Sentence Transformers).

4. Core User Workflows & Key Features

  • Live Incident Triage Queue: Sorts incident feeds dynamically by Composite Priority Score, surfacing imminent danger observations first.
  • Multi-Factor Explainability: Breaks down why an incident was flagged (e.g. "Lockout/Tagout Rule Triggered: Breaker energization without padlock" + "ML High SIF Probability (89%)").
  • Human-in-the-Loop Override: Allows certified HSE Officers to confirm, override, or re-classify risk levels with required justification notes stored permanently in the audit ledger.
  • Semantic Near-Miss Matching: One-click lookup displays previously recorded incidents with similar text patterns, helping investigators detect systemic equipment or contractor failures.
  • Executive KPI Analytics: Aggregates high-potential rate, active review backlogs, and recurring hazard distributions across sites and shifts.
